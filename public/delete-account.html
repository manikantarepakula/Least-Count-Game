Delete Your Account - Least Count

Least Count (developer: Manikanta Repakula) - Last updated: August 24, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Least Count account and the data associated with it.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to manikantarepakula9999@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account".
  2. In the email, include your in-game player name, and your account email if you signed in with Google (if you only ever played as a guest, just your player name and roughly when you played is enough).
  3. We will confirm your identity if needed, then delete your account and associated data within 30 days, and reply once it's done.

What Gets Deleted vs. Kept

Data typeWhat happens
Your player nameDeleted
Firebase account (anonymous ID, or linked Google email/name)Deleted
Game stats (games played, wins)Deleted
Chat messages you sentDeleted
Purchase records (e.g. "Remove Ads")Handled by Google Play -- see Google's own account/purchase history, we only delete our local reference to it
Aggregate, anonymized analytics events (e.g. "a game finished")Kept, but no longer tied to you once your account is deleted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you'd rather just stop using the app without deleting anything, you don't need to do anything -- simply uninstalling the app is enough, though your data will remain stored until you request deletion as above.
