    📜 Game Rules (గేమ్ రూల్స్)

    🎯 Goal (లక్ష్యం)

    Score as few points as possible. When your total points reach the elimination score, you're out. The last player still standing wins.

    🃏 Card Values (కార్డుల విలువ)

    Ace = 1, number cards = face value, face cards (J/Q/K) = 10. Each round, one rank is picked as the Joker — Jokers and that rank are worth 0 points and can replace any card.

    🔄 Your Turn (మీ వంతు)

    Draw a card from the stock pile or the open pile, then discard one card. Try to keep your hand's total value as low as possible.

    🔥 +2 Chain

    Discarding a 2 forces the next player to draw 2 penalty cards — unless they also discard a 2, which stacks the chain higher (+4, +6...). Whoever can't continue it draws the full penalty.

    📣 Declaring (డిక్లేర్)

    Once your hand value is 5 or less, you can declare "Least Count!" If your total really is the lowest at the table, every other player scores their own hand value as penalty points. If you're wrong, you take the penalty instead.

    🏆 Elimination & Winning (ఎలిమినేషన్)

    Points carry over every round. Cross the elimination score (chosen before the game, e.g. 100/200) and you're eliminated. Play continues until only one player remains — they win!
